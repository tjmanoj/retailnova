## Description
```
RetailNova turns every shopper conversation into a demand signal, then turns that signal into action before Diwali.

A Gemini concierge (built with Google's Agent Development Kit) helps shoppers in English, Hindi or Tamil, by text, photo or voice note, and remembers their taste with Agent Platform Memory Bank. It answers only from the real catalog and live stock in every store, and can reserve an item or notify the shopper. Gemini Flash-Lite turns each chat into a structured demand signal, capturing the unmet demand that sales data never sees. The store manager's Demand Radar compares sales-only vs demand-sensed forecasts from BigQuery AI.FORECAST (TimesFM) with a festival calendar. Staff log walk-in requests too. An agent proposes stock transfers and reorders with evidence and Google Search market trends, and one-click approval moves inventory and notifies the waiting shopper with a QR pickup pass. Model Armor screens every prompt.
```
